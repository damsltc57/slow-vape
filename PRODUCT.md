# Slow Vape

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

macOS

## Users

People who want to notice and reduce how often they vape while working at their Mac. The first user has validated the prototype with a Geekvape.

## Product purpose

Count probable puffs from a webcam and help the user act when they reach the limits they set.

## Capabilities and constraints

The app, model, settings and history work offline. Camera frames are processed in memory. The first launch explains detection, collects limits and offers a camera test. The dashboard shows hourly, daily and weekly history. A menu bar icon opens the dashboard. The user explicitly chose a persistent foreground alert with adjustable sound.
